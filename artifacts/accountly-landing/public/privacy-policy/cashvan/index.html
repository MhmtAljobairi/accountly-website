Accountly CashVan Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Accountly ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data through the Accountly CashVan mobile app (the "App"), Android package me.accountly.cashvan, available on Android and iOS.

1. About the App

Accountly CashVan is a business tool for van-sales and field-sales teams that use the Accountly accounting platform. Sales representatives use it to manage customers and visits, record invoices, orders, cash receipts, expenses and inventory movements, and print documents.

Accounts are issued by the company you work for (your "Organization"). Your Organization decides how the App is used and controls the business records entered into it. We process those records on its behalf, and we are responsible for the App itself and for the account, device, and diagnostic data described below.

2. Data We Collect

Account information

Your email address, password (sent in hashed form), name, profile photo if you add one, the company and role you are assigned to, and your salesperson identifier.

Precise location

Your GPS location when you check in at a customer, save or update a customer's location, record a transaction such as an invoice, cash receipt, expense, or inventory movement, or view nearby customers and delivery tours. See section 4.

Business and customer records

Invoices, orders, returns, cash receipts, expenses, inventory transactions, visit records, supervisor notes, and details of your Organization's customers such as names, phone numbers, addresses, and locations.

Photos

Pictures you choose to take with the camera or select from your photo library, for example a profile photo or a photo attached to a client visit or supervisor note. The App uploads only the photos you select and does not scan your library.

Device information

When you sign in: a device identifier (the Android ID, or the identifier for vendor on iOS), operating system and version, manufacturer, device model, and device name. We use these details to secure your account and support your device.

Push notification data

A push subscription token and your account email, used as the identifier that links notifications to your account.

Crash and performance data

Crash reports, error details, performance measurements, device and OS type, and app version, collected to find and fix problems.

Device features that do not send us data

  • Face ID, Touch ID, and fingerprint: verified by your device's operating system. We never receive or store your biometric data.
  • Bluetooth and local network: used to find and connect to receipt printers. Documents go to the printer you choose.
  • Camera for barcodes and QR codes: the code is read on your device, and only its value is used in the App.

We do not access your contacts, and the App does not show advertising.

3. How We Use Data

  • To sign you in, keep your account secure, and apply the permissions your Organization has assigned.
  • To record your work and keep it in sync with your Organization's Accountly account.
  • To verify that visits and transactions took place at the customer's location.
  • To show maps, nearby customers, and delivery tours.
  • To create and print invoices, receipts, and other documents.
  • To send notifications related to your work.
  • To diagnose crashes, improve reliability, and provide support.
  • To meet legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse.

4. Location Data

Location is a core part of the App. It is read at the specific moments listed in section 2 and saved with the related record, so your Organization can verify field activity. The App does not continuously track your location or record your routes, whether it is open or in the background.

You can change or remove location access at any time in your device settings. Check-in, nearby customers, and other location-based features may not work without it.

5. How We Share Data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only as follows:

  • Your Organization: authorized people at your company can see the records you create, including the location saved with them.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run the App, listed below, and our cloud hosting providers.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or when needed to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • Business transfers: if we are involved in a merger or acquisition, under protections equivalent to this policy.

6. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. After your account is closed or a deletion request is completed, we delete or anonymize your personal data within 90 days, unless we must keep it longer for legal, tax, or accounting reasons.

Financial records such as invoices and receipts belong to your Organization and may be kept for the period its legal and accounting obligations require.

7. Requesting Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and personal data, email support@accountly.me from your account email address, with the subject "CashVan account deletion request". You can also ask your Organization's administrator.

We will confirm the request and complete it within the period in section 6. Business records your Organization must legally keep may be retained, with your personal details removed where possible.

8. Data Security

Data is sent between the App and our servers over encrypted HTTPS connections, and access to your Organization's data is limited by account permissions. You can also protect the App with your device's biometric lock. No system is completely secure, so please report any suspected security problem to support@accountly.me.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, contact us at support@accountly.me. Because accounts are managed by your Organization, we may need to involve it or refer some requests to it.

You can turn off location, camera, photo, Bluetooth, and notification permissions in your device settings at any time. Some features may stop working as a result.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for working adults and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date, and we will notify you in the App or by email if the changes are significant.
